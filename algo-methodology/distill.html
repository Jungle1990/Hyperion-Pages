The Whole Methodology on One Screen

一屏看尽算法之道

If you remember only this page, you remember most of what every great algorithm book is trying to teach. 如果只记一页，就记这一页——它几乎包含了所有伟大算法书想教的事。

🧩 Design 设计范式

  • Brute force is the baseline — never skip it暴力是基线，别跳过
  • Divide & conquer when sub-problems are independent子问题独立时用分治
  • DP when sub-problems overlap — name the state子问题重叠时用 DP，关键在命名状态
  • Greedy when you can prove an exchange argument能证明交换论证时用贪心
  • Backtracking + bound when search is structured搜索结构化时用回溯+剪枝
  • Randomize to neutralize adversarial input随机化抵消对抗输入
  • Reduce to a problem you already solved归约到已会的问题

📐 Analyze 分析

  • O / Θ / Ω hide constants — for large N only渐近记号只在大 N 有效
  • Solve recurrences: Master · Tree · Akra–Bazzi递推求解：主定理 · 递归树 · Akra–Bazzi
  • Amortize a sequence — not the worst single op分析整个序列，而非最坏一步
  • Adversary arguments prove lower bounds对手论证给出下界
  • Empirical doubling: T(2N)/T(N) → growth exponent实验倍增：比值给出指数

🛠️ Engineer 工程

  • Estimate orders of magnitude BEFORE coding写代码前先估量级
  • Reformulate the problem — the real win is upstream重述问题——真正的杠杆在上游
  • Simplicity over cleverness (Hoare)简单优于聪明
  • Profile, then optimize (Knuth)先剖析，再优化
  • Prove correctness with loop invariants用循环不变量证明正确性
  • Augment, don't replace — add a field to a known DS增强而非重造——给已知结构加字段

🕰️ Remember 铭记

  • 1946 Mauchly — Binary search梅奥赫利 · 二分查找
  • 1953 Bellman — Dynamic programming贝尔曼 · 动态规划
  • 1959 Hoare — Quicksort · Dijkstra — SSSP霍尔/迪杰斯特拉
  • 1965 Cooley–Tukey — FFTFFT
  • 1971 Cook · 1972 Karp — NP-completenessNP 完全性
  • 1976 Rabin — Randomized algorithms随机化算法登堂入室
  • 1985 Tarjan — Amortization (potential method)塔扬 · 摊还分析

🧠 Ask Yourself 扪心自问

  • What's the simplest non-trivial instance?最小非平凡实例长什么样？
  • What invariant is preserved? What monovariant changes?有什么不变量？什么单调量？
  • Is this an old problem in disguise? Can I reduce?这是旧问题的伪装吗？能归约吗？
  • What does the extreme element look like?极端元素长什么样？
  • Is feasibility monotone? Can I binary-search the answer?可行性单调吗？能否二分答案？
  • Have I measured? Or am I just guessing?我测量过了吗？还是只是在猜？
